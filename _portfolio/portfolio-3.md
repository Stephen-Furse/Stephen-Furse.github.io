If you do want to be able to play this game you can find it [here](https://static-eulogy-studios.itch.io).

Mechanics included in the game and made by me; player controller, multiple weapon attack types (melee, shotgun style, throwables, rapid fire), ammo count, weapon pickups (stackable), simple follow enemy AI, wave spawning with scaling difficulty, randomised enemy sounds per weighted system, enemy hurt sounds, All of the UI features.

This game was made as part of end of course game jam that the lecturers do at City College Norwich so that they can have something to show off to future years of our works but also so they have something that can truly show off all of our progress, this was made with most of the class of 18 with some people of that choosing not to participate which was fine as it wasn't a mandatory thing for the course more sort of a thing you can do if you'd like, but as one of the first few years of this course starting seperate from animation, I felt it was only right to make something for them which i did.

Intially we all got together as a class with a few of us stood a the front coming up with ideas and asking what people liked or what they would change to come up to a comon consenus of a game idea that we would all be happy with making together. That is how we ended up on making a wave shooter based in a supermarket, with self checkouts attacking you, so you defend yourself with the shops stock of food items. 

This was made in Godot 4.5 and was made in collaboration with another programmer in my class Leo Walsh, who handled some of the programming aspects for things like the shopping trolley, how it interacts with the consumables and the things necessary to finishing the game.
