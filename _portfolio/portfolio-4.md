This game was made as a project for my end of course at City College Norwich where we as a class of around 20 came together to make a game for them to show to new starters. This game was a consolidation of everything that I had learnt from my time at college and putting it all to use. It was made in collaboration with another class mate for all the programming and was made in Godot 4.5 using GD Script. 

Mechanics that I worked on: Player controller, multiple weapon types, ammo amount, different attack types, weapon pick ups, enemy AI.
