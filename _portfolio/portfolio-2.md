If you do want to be able to play this game you can find it [here](https://static-eulogy-studios.itch.io).

Mechanics included in the game; First Person Shooter system (reloading, aiming, gun switching, melee all animated as well), enemy spawning in area at random, different variants of enemies, weighted randomisation buff system, points per kill system (based on enemy).

This was by far one of the biggest projects that I've done to date (12/11/2026) taking over 4 months of work, hundreds of hours in engine, tens of hours of tutorials and debugging. Purely from adding in all the features to all the map design to all of the seperate enemy variants (made poorly but made nonetheless) and this is a project I still hold in my heart quite dearly as it was one that i had a lot of fun with, being given creative freedom to just make was and is one of the best choices for a final project for college, this allowed me to work with other classmates essentialy creating a mini game studio of sorts and getting them to work one seperate things like models for characters or props but then also getting an artist in the draw me up a poster from a simple reference poster making my life easier by giving me more time to sit there and program everything rather than taking hours away to make the things i needed making. 

The main things that I learnt from this project was a heavy amount of time management. But also how to use Unreal Engine 5 effectively and efficiently using most of its capabilities including landscaping tools, parenting blueprints, weighted randomisation systems, how to make simple ai's, how to make a cutscene, 3D triggerboxes, map design and key parts of what makes a good map, enemy wave spawning with difficulty scaling, a checkpoint system for the story mode only allowing certain parts to be accesible at a given time, asyncronhous loading to make the levels load faster with a loading screen.
