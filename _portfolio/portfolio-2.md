This project was made in college and was intended to be carried on over the summer but I still haven't gotten round to it; So currently its still in its intial alpha version. This project mostly helped me get a solid grasp on Unreal Engine 4 and 5.

Over the course of the game I learnt how to implement lots of different mechanics ranging from an intricate FPS shooter system; including reloading, aiming, gun switching. It also included other things like various zombie variants and zombie spawning but also a randomized buff system.
